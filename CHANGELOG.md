# [1.0.0] — 2026-09-01

## Foundational Release

The first public release of **General Structure Unfolding Intelligence (GSUI)**.

This release establishes GSUI as a general structural research framework for studying how persistent structures produce, constrain, evaluate, preserve, and incorporate runtime structural variation.

The foundational structural form is:

$$
S_{t+1}=S_t\pm\Delta_t
$$

or conceptually:

```text
<Core Structure> +/- <Delta Modification>
```

The release develops this minimal form into a broader intelligence framework involving:

```text
Folding
→ Core Structure
→ Triggering
→ Unfolding Space
→ Delta Modification
→ Control
→ Evaluation
→ Certification
→ Feedback
→ Structural Growth
→ Updated Structure
```

## Added — Foundational Theory

### Folding and Unfolding

Introduced **Folding** and **Unfolding** as complementary phases of a broader Intelligence Cycle.

```text
World
→ Observation
→ Data
→ Folding
→ Structure
→ Unfolding
→ Prediction / Reasoning / Planning / Generation / Action
→ World
```

Established the research question:

> **After intelligence forms structure, how does that structure become runtime intelligence?**

---

### Core Structure and Delta Modification

Introduced the canonical GSUI structural form:

```text
Core Structure +/- Delta Modification
```

Established Delta as a general structural modification rather than simple addition.

Candidate Delta operations include:

```text
Preserve
Add
Delete
Replace
Branch
Merge
Reconnect
Reweight
Instantiate
Sample
Activate
Deactivate
Move
Compress
Expand
```

Clarified that Core Structure is **relatively persistent at the current analytical scale**, not necessarily permanently immutable.

---

### General Structure Unfolding Intelligence

Introduced **General Structure Unfolding Intelligence (GSUI)** as a cross-domain structural framework.

Foundational definition:

> **Structure Unfolding Intelligence is the capacity of a structured system to produce, select, evaluate, and incorporate context-conditioned structural variations while preserving relevant core invariants.**

Compressed formulation:

> **Intelligence is controlled structural unfolding under constraints.**

Established the methodological boundary:

> **Structural comparability does not imply algorithmic identity.**

---

### Generation as Unfolding

Introduced the conceptual relation:

$$
Generation \subset Unfolding
$$

Generation is treated as one important form of structural unfolding rather than as the complete unfolding problem.

## Added — Unfolding Space

Introduced **Unfolding Space**:

$$
\mathcal{U}(S,C)
$$

as the structured set of candidate transitions available from a structure under context.

Established the hierarchy:

$$
\mathcal{U}_{selected}
\subseteq
\mathcal{U}_{preferred}
\subseteq
\mathcal{U}_{allowed}
\subseteq
\mathcal{U}_{reachable}
\subseteq
\mathcal{U}_{possible}
$$

Introduced the distinction:

```text
Can
≠
May
≠
Should
≠
Will
```

Established separate concepts for:

* Possible Unfolding;
* Reachable Unfolding;
* Allowed Unfolding;
* Preferred Unfolding;
* Selected Unfolding;
* Certified Unfolding.

---

## Added — Search vs Unfolding

Established an explicit distinction between **Search** and **Structural Unfolding**.

Search generally selects candidates within an existing possibility space.

Structural Unfolding may modify the structure that generates future possibility itself.

Therefore:

$$
S_t \rightarrow S_{t+1}
$$

may produce:

$$
\mathcal{U}(S_t)
\neq
\mathcal{U}(S_{t+1})
$$

This distinction becomes a foundation for the GSUI treatment of Structural Growth.

## Added — Structural Certification

Introduced **Structural Certification** as a mechanism for governing consequential unfolding.

For:

$$
S' = S + \Delta S
$$

GSUI asks:

```text
What is ΔS?

Was ΔS authorized?

Does ΔS preserve required invariants?

Was ΔS faithfully implemented?

Should the resulting structure be allowed to cross
the consequential action boundary?
```

CallingGraph-based AI Coding is identified as an important concrete prototype for this approach.

## Added — Structural Growth

Defined **Structural Growth** as:

> **Retained unfolding that changes future unfolding capacity.**

Established:

```text

```

```text

```

```text
Structural Growth
```

Structural Growth may expand, contract, compress, reorganize, redirect, or constrain future Unfolding Space.

# Foundational Statements of v1.0.0

The release establishes six central statements.

### 1.

> **Intelligence does not begin only with computation over data; it can also be studied as the unfolding of structure.**

### 2.

> **A broad class of intelligent processes can be represented as `<Core Structure> +/- <Delta Modification>`.**

### 3.

> **Generation is one form of unfolding; unfolding is broader than generation.**

### 4.

> **Intelligent unfolding requires not only possibility, but triggering, constraint, policy, evaluation, feedback, and invariant preservation.**

### 5.

> **Human-defined intelligence categories may eventually compress into a much smaller set of general unfolding primitives.**

### 6.

> **Structural Self may be understood as the persistent reference structure relative to which unfolding and continuity are defined.**

---

# Release Boundary

GSUI v1.0.0 is intentionally a **theory-first foundational release**.

It does not yet attempt to provide:

* a complete GSUI mathematical algebra;
* a universal GSUI runtime;
* a complete primitive instruction set;
* a comprehensive experimental benchmark;
* a proof of Unfolding Universality;
* a complete formal theory of Structural Self;
* or a production implementation of autonomous Meta-Unfolding.

These remain future research directions.

The purpose of v1.0.0 is to establish the conceptual foundation clearly enough that these questions can now be investigated systematically.

# Research Transition Opened by v1.0.0

The foundational transition is:

```text
Folding
→ Structure
→ Unfolding
```

The deeper transition is:

```text
Structure
→ Possible Futures
→ Controlled Unfolding
→ Consequential Change
→ Feedback
→ Structural Growth
→ New Possible Futures
```

Therefore, one of the central GSUI research transitions is:

$$
\mathcal{U}(S_t)
\rightarrow
\mathcal{U}(S_{t+1})
$$

The first-order problem is changing a state.

The deeper problem is changing the structure of future possibility.
